# Definition of Done: where the project stands

The design ends with a "ready for production" checklist: each box must be
proven by an artefact of the repository. This page goes through it honestly,
on 13 November 2026, five days after continuous collection started.

**Done** = proven by the linked artefact. **Partial** = part of the box is
proven, the rest is said. **Not done** = not started, or replaced (said how).

## Data

| Box | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Collector in continuous service since week 1, < 1 % missed minutes, gaps listed and explained | Partial | Running since 8 Nov; silent gaps 0.00-0.63 % per day on 8-10 Nov, the 5 gaps of 9 Nov explained ([quality, R6](data/quality.md#r6-two-kinds-of-missed-versions)); reports to rerun on every day since; live health on the Admin page |
| Station reference historised; rebalancing and frozen periods detected, error rate measured on a sample | Partial | Frozen feed and frozen stations detected (R4a, R4b, [quality](data/quality.md)); reference history (R5) and rebalancing detection not done |

| Daily Parquet archive, versioned by DVC, publishable under ODbL | Partial | Daily Parquet ([storage](data/storage.md)); not versioned by DVC |

## Summary

Of 25 boxes: 6 done, 15 partial, 2 not done or replaced, the 4 weeks in
progress, the video to record. What is missing most is *time*: four weeks of data would
rerun every result above, make the profiles meaningful and decide whether the
model beats persistence. The bench, the monitoring and the service are in
place to measure it.
